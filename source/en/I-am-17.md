My 17th birthday is almost over~ Happy birthday to me! :-D

**Update, January 3, 2015:**

This was my 17th birthday. Looking back now, I was childish and ridiculous, but that was the real me. If I could go back to that day, I would tell myself: go back, go back to class, go back to school, sit in the classroom and listen properly to a teacher all the way through one lesson.
