My plan to study abroad has been canceled. There are a lot of reasons, so I won't go into exactly why. These past two years were like the ocean, and then suddenly a meteorite crashed into it and threw everything into chaos. Of course my dream is still there, and I have to keep going. There's nothing much to celebrate at Christmas. I'm on my own, and I still feel so lonely. It's cold and bleak. Finally, Merry Christmas, everyone. Next year I need to prepare seriously for the gaokao, the college entrance exam, so I'll have less and less time to chat with you all about blogs.
