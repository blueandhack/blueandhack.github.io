Lots of blogs have started posting their year-end reviews, and I've been torn. This year I had to make a hard decision: take a leave from school or not, sit the gaokao or not. In the end I chose to take the leave and skip the gaokao.<!-- more -->

What did I gain this year? What did I lose? What do I still want? What will I lose next? I don't feel like talking about it. Wind, let it all fly away.

2011, let me make a wish. Yeah, let it sprout in my heart and grow into a big tree.

In 2011 I'm going to sleep on time, get up on time, eat on time, and limit my time online every day. My free time is slowly shrinking, and if I go to another city for classes next year, I'll be online even less. Wind, I'll follow wherever you go.

I was born on September 9, 1993. Without that day, there'd be no me today.

In 1995, if I remember right, I lived in Beijing for half a year. It's a blurry memory… I can't really recall it anymore.

In 1999 I started first grade, and just like that, without knowing it, my life as a student began

In 2005 I finished elementary school and started middle school, a clueless kid

In 2008 I finished middle school and started high school, still a clueless kid

In 2010, I'm alive…

And I'll keep on living…

(PS: Anyone who tries to social-engineer me is dead meat. Watch out, or you'll get a steel pipe where the sun don't shine~)

Lastly, I've changed my pen name. It used to be "Jianfeng Yiyu," and now it's "Yuzhe." Normally my posts will go out under "blueandhack," but of course… the ones I'm really proud of will be signed Yuzhe. Ugh, I'm just being silly now…

I won't show off my signature…

2011, the girlfriend thing: all the friends around me have one. I think~ I'll just forget it… I've got no luck with girls~~ I'll go off and mind my own business somewhere. To the guys reading this~ I might not make it back for the graduation photo next year, though who knows. Just remember to save me a spot. Ahem~ just save me a spot where you can paste me in. Hopefully I can make it.

THE END. Happy New Year, everyone!
