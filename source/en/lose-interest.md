It's been a long time since I wrote on this blog. The passion I used to have has faded with time. Friends who used to blog have slowly moved on to technical stuff, and I don't plan to make themes anymore either. In the end, simpler really is better. Why make things hard on myself? So be it~ This blog will rarely be updated from now on. I'll be writing more on our couple's blog instead. I don't want to share the address. It's a little world that belongs to just her and me~
