A poem for myself:<!-- more -->

Over the vast sea

He beats his wings and flies

In the boundless sky

He gazes at the distant horizon

In the empty air

His companions are the clouds, the wind and the waves. One belief lives in his mind

I will fly, fly to that faraway shore

He grows a little tired

Slowly he sinks toward the surface of the sea

Splash……

A wave soaks his wings

He wobbles, as if about to vanish in the battering sea

He shakes out his wings

Pulls himself together

Changes his posture

And climbs high into the sky

The sea wind brushes the scales on his body

Their old brilliance has faded. He seems to have forgotten that he was once just a sturdy butterfly

Forgotten the mockery and the sneers of others. He remembers only his old dream

Today's goal

Someday

When he gathers nectar in the gardens on the far shore

He will beat his wings and fly, happy
