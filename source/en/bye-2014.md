Time slips by so fast that I can't catch hold of either end of it. By the time I come back to my senses, it's gone. Looking back on my 2014, it just feels like it vanished in a rush.<!-- more -->

My 2014 was split in two: one part in China, one part in the US.

Looking back on 2014, I went to Bengbu, Hefei, Rizhao, Hangzhou, Shanghai, Los Angeles, Phoenix and Tucson.

Let me look back on the part in China.

I never had a complete high school experience, and now I don't have a complete college experience either. After only two years together, my college life in China was over.

## 01

This section is my summer vacation. I don't know if it counts as my last summer vacation in China. The whole vacation was only a month long. During that month I went to Shanghai for my visa interview, then took driving lessons and passed the second part of the driving test, only to realize that I'd basically wasted the whole summer.

On August 11, I set off for Shanghai, with my parents along. After eating my last meal in China in 2014 at the Ajisen Ramen at Pudong Airport, I walked through the departure checkpoint without looking back. My dad told me my mom cried that day after I left. At the time I wasn't sad at all, probably too excited. It was only after arriving in the US that I slowly started to get homesick.

## 10

In Tucson I met a bunch of buddies who give me a hard time but also really look out for me. We all have a great time together. I'm pretty happy with how things are now.

![](https://cdn.blueandhack.com/pics/WP_20140812_08_36_53_Pro.jpg)

My school, UA

Actually, during my time in the US, going from bursting with novelty to now being homesick, missing the food from home, there's a feeling I really can't put into words. It's bottled up in my heart and won't go away. I remember it was about a month after I came to the US. One night I was making pig's trotter soup at home, and while I was making it I thought of how my mom used to make me pig's trotter soup every time I came home from out of town. I couldn't stop the tears, and I cried the whole night. I've also regretted it, asking myself why I came here, but in the end I still told myself: it was my dream, and even if I have to crawl, I'll crawl all the way to the end.

Coming to the US brought a lot of firsts: the first time cutting my own hair, the first time bowling, the first time cooking for someone else, the first time moving...

Of course, I guess only by going through all this do you really grow up.

So much happened this year. Sometimes I feel the year went by too slowly, making me live through so much turmoil. Sometimes I feel it went by too fast, ending before I could really savor the life in it. Maybe that's just life. My 2014, goodbye, I'll never see you again. Maybe I'll be grateful to this year for making me a lot more mature and helping me grow up quite a bit.

## Finally

Maybe you'll ask me how I'm doing now. I'll tell you "good," but the real pain is something only I know. Treat happiness as life, and treat troubles as seasoning. Only by fighting your way through life do you become mature and steady. Hello, 2015, here I come.
