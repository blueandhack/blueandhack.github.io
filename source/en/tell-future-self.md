I know time doesn't pass that quickly, but I want to leave myself a little reminder here: it's time to fight for my dream with everything I've got. Tonight I took a short nap, like I do every day, dragging my tired body along. I want to fight, not rest. What I hope is that a year from today I'll be sitting somewhere different, on the opposite side of the world from this country, learning different things. What I hope for will take my hard work.
