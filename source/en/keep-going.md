Updated this blog tonight. I hadn't looked at it in ages. Every dorm room always has a pig, you just don't know who the pig is until you move in. Since coming to this school, there's been too much restlessness, too much self-importance. I don't know if I'm an island, and my personality has always been a little odd. I can't see myself through other people's eyes, so I don't know where I stand. I wanted a new theme, so I started writing code and debugging. I'm tired of this theme. This semester I've been studying C, computer technology, and advanced math. I'd like to sum them up on this blog, bit by bit. At least this blog should stick around for a while and let me update it slowly, one post a day. I'll write tutorials when I have time, turning what's in the books into my own words for everyone. That counts as some value for this blog, I suppose. I can't just moan about nothing. I'm not dead yet. I want to do something for the people around me, I want to do something for the world. Okay, enough ranting. Time for me to turn the lights out too. Lights out is always right at 11:30 every night. Good night, everyone. I hope someone still reads my blog. Heh, even though it's mostly about my own life, if you follow me, leave me a comment, to prove that I'm alive and that you're alive too.
