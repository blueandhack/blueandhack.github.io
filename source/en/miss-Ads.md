Last night I turned on my computer for once (I've been busy studying lately) and opened my Gmail. There was an email from feedsky sitting in my inbox

![image](https://cdn.blueandhack.com/images/2010/04/miss-Ads/image_thumb20.png)

<!-- more -->Topic marketing. I opened it and logged into the dashboard~ um~ I got the opportunity, but all the slots were already taken~

![image](https://cdn.blueandhack.com/images/2010/04/miss-Ads/image_thumb21.png)

The Accept button was grayed out~

20 yuan, heh, gone. But I hear feedsky's ads are mostly the "chicken essence" kind of junk, so I don't really care about those scammy-looking ADs anyway~ Maybe next time if I get the chance~

Now about Google ads. I think this is the 10th time I've been rejected, and here's this time's

![image](https://cdn.blueandhack.com/images/2010/04/miss-Ads/image_thumb22.png)

> We did not approve your application for the following reasons.   

Issue:   

- Difficult site navigation: When reviewing the site you submitted, www.blueandhack.com, we were unable to access your site. Your site may be down, or it may contain software that could harm users' computers.

I'm bummed, and now I get it. 10 times. I applied over and over in a row, so it seems the Google reviewer lost patience and gave me a reason like this~ and gave it to me twice...........

I've completely lost confidence in messing with this stuff~ I'll just keep the blog focused on writing~ And then there's Baidu Union, 3 failures there too~ ugh~ forget about it

The participation certificate for Earth Hour 2010 from a few weeks ago landed in my inbox, an e-certificate from WWF:

![image](https://cdn.blueandhack.com/images/2010/04/miss-Ads/image_thumb23.png)

These few things taught me: what's missed doesn't come back, so seize the present and don't hesitate over your choices. You only learn to treasure something once it's gone~ a lesson everyone seems to understand~
