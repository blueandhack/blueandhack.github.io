My first time meeting venture capitalists. I went to Los Angeles with a friend for my dream. Thinking about the days behind me, wasted and aimless, I got in touch with friends back in China and spent a few weeks building a demo, then brought it along. And then the big shots took one look and said: the demo isn't important. What's important is, have you figured out what you want to do? Have you figured out your business model? Yeah, that's what I learned.
