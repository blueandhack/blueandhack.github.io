I'm watching my college career come to an end before it even really began. Whatever else I can say, I've gained a lot in these two years. Right now I'm in Hefei, doing a training program at iFlytek Education.

<!-- more -->

### Waiting

This is the second half of my sophomore year. I came to Hefei disappointed and feeling lost. Deep down I didn't want to come here, but I had no choice. Everything here is just awful. Apart from the food and housing being a bit better, everything else is basically the same as before.

### Choice

My classmates around me are all playing LOL or other games. I rarely play games myself, and I rarely ask them why they love playing so much. For them, maybe games really are the only place they can find any fun. They haven't grown up inside. Maybe it's just a different worldview, or maybe that's why they ended up here. Like frogs at the bottom of a well, they only look at the patch of sky above them and have no wish to climb any higher.

### Hope

I've decided to take my own road. I hope I can succeed and keep going.
