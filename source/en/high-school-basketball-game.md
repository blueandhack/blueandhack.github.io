This all happened a year ago and I'm still bringing it up. I'm clueless about sports and don't really follow basketball. I do play sometimes, but I very, very rarely watch. Still, this is a rare video record of high school life. It was the basketball tournament the school held last March. Sadly, we just missed out on first and second place. Ours was the only class that went into overtime against another class, in the semifinal. Sigh~ in a word: tragic. Our captain got sent off, then overtime started~ and with him went the half of the sky he'd been holding up~ so overtime wasn't much to watch, but our class gave it everything! We took close to 1,000 photos over the whole tournament~ but only recorded video of the last game, the match for third place. The footage was 3GB in total, a huge file. I wanted to upload it to Tudou back then, but Tudou had limits at the time, so I kept putting it off until, finally, today. It took me a whole day, but the video is finally uploaded~ HOHO~!!<!-- more -->
