After school started, instead of classes we began a practical training program, developing a Java project in small teams. I'm team lead again, and I'm going to be teaching people again too. It feels pretty good. I've felt a lot this past year; after all, I mean to keep this blog as a diary. With all the code I've written along the way, I wonder if it would wrap around the Earth. I hope I keep growing every day. I passed the English Level B exam with an 82, and I need to keep working at it. I know what I want to do, and I'm lucky.
