A few days ago CN WORDPRESS changed hands. (I've confirmed he's a middle school graduate. Sigh, now I feel like I've missed so many opportunities, so much time.) <strike>Apparently the new guy is a high school student, or a middle school student, or a college student, not sure about his age, most likely a high school student, whatever~ Anyway, he's not someone with a job,</strike> Great, the once lifeless CN WORDPRESS finally has some energy. His taking over might well bring WordPress in China back to life~ o(∩_∩)o haha<!-- more -->

Him: [Zheng Jiehan](http://jiehan.org) ([a new high school freshman](https://jiehan.org/life/wordpress-3-0-1-extra-and-my-class-presiden/)). I don't know him, but I bow down to him. He's taken on the heavy job of releasing updates for CN WORDPRESS. Thanks to him. Paveo, who did it before, also deserves respect.

[The official Chinese WORDPRESS 3.0.1 update is out](http://cn.wordpress.org/2010/08/14/wordpress-3-0-1/). I waited a long, long time...

There's also a [WordPress 3.0.1 supplementary language pack update](http://cn.wordpress.org/2010/08/16/wordpress-3-0-1-extra/). That's the new energy for you~

I really did get into building websites late. What was I doing in seventh grade? In eighth grade? In ninth grade? Sigh, what a tragedy, so much time just slipped away. The person localizing WORDPRESS and taking it over is a middle schooler. Ugh, I feel so inferior...

Sigh, it was always my mom holding me back, not giving me money to build a site. Only in 2010 did I finally get some money, and that's when I built my site. I got into this so, so late. Sigh, really. I've missed a lot of chances, and now I can only make up for it with hard work.

Finally, thanks to [Zheng Jiehan](http://jiehan.org) for bringing new energy to CN WORDPRESS. Another tech whiz. Thanks for all your hard work.

PS: Speaking of which, I'm learning C++, so why does it feel like there's nowhere to use it... At my level I'd never make it into NOIP...
