Today took the whole day. We started shooting at 1:30 p.m. and finished at 6:30 p.m., 5 hours in total. It's only a short film a few minutes long, but we wanted plenty of footage for post-production, so we took it slow and did it right.<!-- more -->  By the time I got back to the dorm today I was completely wiped out, and tomorrow I still have to prepare a speech. It feels like every day I've got something going on. I'm worried all day long. My world is missing happiness. Sometimes, when I feel awful, I want to stay somewhere quiet, listen to music and enjoy my own world, but neither space nor time allows it. All I can do is sink into myself. I like solitude. I like my own world, where I have room to think. I don't like playing online games. I like reading, I like watching the movies I want to watch. I'm really, really tired. I want to rest. I want to go home. I miss Mom and Dad, I miss home. As for this place, I'm slowly getting sick of it. I'm sad, I'm down. I want to go back. I'll share the film with everyone once the editing is done.
