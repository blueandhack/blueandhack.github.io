Tonight I steeled myself and removed some links from my blogroll. Mostly they were blogs I never interacted with, or blogs that had nothing to do with mine. A link exchange should be friendship first, link second. That's a mistake I didn't realize I was making in the first half of the year, when I added links without really thinking. Now, if you see this notice, please take my link down too. I've already left messages for some of you; for the rest, if you see this, go ahead and remove it.  The links I removed are: <!-- more -->
