A lot of things keep happening around me, and they're what got this post written. I've been thinking about a lot lately. After all, this is my senior year of high school: junior year stayed behind in June, and senior year is finally here.<!-- more --> A few months ago I was still talking about what to do in senior year, and now I really have to face it. The goofing-around days, the easygoing days, the happy-go-lucky days are basically all gone. A heavy subject: senior-year me, which road should I take? Road one, the gaokao, but I've already lost hope there. I resented the gaokao all through my first two years, so I'm bad at every high school subject. Road two, going abroad. That looks like a tough road too, but I'm confident about it; my English scores are the problem, though. Road three, art. That's what Mom suggested, and I really don't want to, but art, drawing, was my original strength, and I could easily get good results in drawing. Three roads, and right now I'm choosing the second. I'm taking the TOEFL on August 21, but I don't know how to do well on it. Mom says if I don't get 80, I'll have to go study art. I'm so lost……  Maybe in a few days I'll go to New Oriental; Mom wants me to take classes there. Yesterday I finally got the domain transfer code and moved my domain to Godaddy. I'd wanted to do that three months ago, and finally my dream came true. I want to thank Chen Ye here. A few days ago my hosting got suspended in the US for using too many server resources, so my account was disabled and the site went down. Once again he helped me get it unsuspended, moved my data out, and transferred it to another host. No data was lost at all. So here's a shout-out: [Qingyun Hosting](http://kisscloud.com), a great-value host. School's out. To prepare for the exam I applied to the school for a leave, so I'm staying home and will have time to write posts. A few days ago I gave my blog a definition: from now on I'll mainly write basic C++ tutorials and basic English tutorials, plus some anime resources. Focusing a blog on one or a few topics is also an optimization as far as search engine indexing goes. From now on, two posts a day. I'm going to keep that habit~~~ About my flaws: I'm way too lazy. I kept putting this post off, and it took 3 days to finish~ No more school; see you in senior year. Everything is going to be great. There are still some things I'm not happy with in this theme, and I keep tweaking it. Look forward to my new theme~ I'm going to make this theme blue and white with bigger fonts. It'll look seriously cool! Memorizing vocab is going to give me a headache. What I absolutely have to finish over the break is memorizing the TOEFL and GRE word lists. I've written up a plan, and it should take about a month. Painful, huh~~ OK, that's some of what happened yesterday, today and tomorrow, what I need to do and what I need to fix……
