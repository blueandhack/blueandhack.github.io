I've been way too busy. Actually, a lot of the time it's busy for nothing: I don't do things properly, so it's really just wasting time<!-- more -->

I can't find the feeling I had a year ago anymore. A year ago I was still a kid, I guess……

Lately I've been reading Plato's The Republic

Its Chinese title is Lixiangguo, "The Ideal State"

Heh~~ it's the English version, so it's a bit of a struggle to read~ but it really draws you in. It's one conversation after another~

I once wrote a post on my QQ Zone called "Everything Tied to Politics Is Ugly"

Then, not long after, I decided that kind of commentary was pointless and deleted it

Yes, it really is pointless. Focus on my own studies and everything else, and walk my own road

I hope I can wander between the nation and the government~ Utopia~~

I recently bought a mouse pad. I didn't check the model when I bought it, and when it arrived, WOW, it was huge

Probably because I only figured out what on earth C++ was a week before the exam~
