This theme has been brewing for a really long time, and today I finally squeezed it out~

<!-- more -->

Since it's the middle of the night I won't go into much detail. This theme does away with a lot of plugins, or more precisely, it saves a lot of memory~ After all I'm on a VPS~ not some dedicated physical server~ so I save wherever I can~ Newbies don't know how to save~ so I'll help them out a bit. Don't pile on too many plugins~ or your server keeps crashing~ and snap, there goes your blog!

Why is the theme called WHITE LOVE?

Well, actually, it reminds me of her~ Not saying her name~ hehe~

This theme isn't really original~ it's just a patchwork of several themes~

I tweaked it for 3 days and finally finished. The background is swiped from Tencent Weibo~ hehe. Tencent didn't even set up hotlink protection~ so I'll just hotlink it! Haha~~ I'm a QQ VIP member and I've given Tencent so much money, so they ought to let me use a picture or two, right~~

I'm planning to keep this theme for my own use~~ It's my very first theme~ so pure~~

o(∩_∩)o haha

A quick rundown of the features: AJAX comment replies, Ctrl+Enter quick submit, plugin-free threaded email reply notifications, lazy-loaded images, plugin-free related posts, and so on...

It'll definitely get more complete from here! OK, that's it for now. Off to bed!~~
