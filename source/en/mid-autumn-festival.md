This morning I was groggy, and my stomach suddenly hurt like crazy~~ went to the bathroom to deal with it~~ turned out to be diarrhea. Then I slept until 10, got up, read a bit and went online.<!-- more -->

After lunch I got in touch with Hu Di and said we should go see *Under the Hawthorn Tree*. At first he agreed. I said 4 o'clock, and he said OK.

A little after three in the afternoon I was in the shower, rubbing shampoo all over my head, when suddenly the water got cut off…… Ugh, how awkward. I yelled for my dad, and we used the house's backup water supply, a kettle of water we'd stored earlier, to get through it. I barely managed to finish my shower.

Then I sat around waiting for Hu Di to call. At 4 he called and said he wasn't coming. Talk about a facepalm. Luckily I hadn't bought the tickets yet……

After that I said we could switch movies, and he said he wanted to see *Inception*…… Didn't we just see it a few days ago?…… Hu Di was doing it for some girl…… I was speechless……

So I bought a ticket by myself and watched the movie with two girls. And then…… before the movie started, I ran into Tao Xue, then Huang Mengjia, then Gao Di…… Huainan sure is a small place.

Getting my hair cut in the afternoon was even more awkward. I waited about half an hour. If I'd known, I would've just picked a stylist…… damn…… wasted so much time!

The movie was really long~ and really slow, so by the end it only moved me a tiny bit. I still remember that other film, called something like *Your and My First Love*, a Japanese film. If it had been in Chinese it would've been pretty good. They're all tragic love stories.

Sigh, it's left me really tired now. I want to sleep.

Even after getting home I can't go online. I have to memorize vocabulary first.

Read my English book. Right now there are only three things in my head:

1.English

2.Programming and algorithms

3.Websites

Everything else isn't essential anymore~
