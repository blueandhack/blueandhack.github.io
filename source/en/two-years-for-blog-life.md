For a long, long time, I disappeared. For the gaokao, I can pick things up and put them down, and I've seen through it all. If I have to take the exam, I'll take it. I chose this, so I'll give it my best. This site is two years old now, but I'm not in the mood to hold any kind of celebration. A while ago I renewed the domain. I think I'll just keep renewing it forever. It'll stay around, the same as me.
