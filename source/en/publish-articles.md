First, to put it modestly: two of my articles got published.<!-- more -->I only saw the actual magazine half a month later……  Here's the thing: hardly any newsstands around here sell Computer Application Digest…… usually they have one or two copies, and they're basically gone in two days…… Also, since my articles were pretty low-IQ (the titles are below; one look and you'll see just how low-IQ), the magazine didn't send me a complimentary copy…… so I had to buy one myself…… I ordered the magazine online a few days ago, and it only arrived today. For the record, it's Computer Application Digest, 2010 issue 27, pages 42 and 43. …… For 17 years I've had a dream that one day my writing would appear in a magazine…… and today it came true…… It was published under my nickname, blueandhack. Which reminds me of something from the past. Back in middle school, a teacher confiscated one of my computer magazines, jabbed a finger at my forehead and said: "Do you even understand this stuff? This is just a little side dish. Don't waste your study time on it." Today I've made it the road I'm walking in life, and I hope you can see that. Enough talk, here are the photos
