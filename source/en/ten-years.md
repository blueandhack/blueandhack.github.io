It's been almost four years since I came to the US. Early yesterday morning I drove my "son" to the airport for his flight back to China, and it suddenly hit me how fast time flies. The guy who used to get picked up and dropped off at the airport is now the one driving other people there, and the guy who never liked driving now drives himself to school and work.
