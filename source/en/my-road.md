Mom said once I've made a solid plan I can take time off from school. I said I'd think about it<!-- more -->

For me, school is somewhere I really don't want to go. The teachers are good, my classmates are good, it's just the system that's no good. Choosing to take time off is the smart move~

But then again, if I bomb the TOEFL, I'm finished. Would I repeat junior year, or keep going into senior year? I don't know.

So what I have to do is take the TOEFL twice, and the last one has to be a pass!

My own goal~ a score of 100 or higher.

My skills, o(∩_∩)o hahaha!!!

Today I was practicing speaking on Skype and got disconnected because Mom needed the internet to file taxes. Um~ We were only halfway through, and it was just my turn to talk. Um~

I've been really tired lately. The moment I hit the bed I'm out cold~ No idea why. And then I wake up right on time

Last Saturday I was late for school and they called my parents in. That day I got up, walked to the bus stop, looked, and was shocked: road work, closed off. I was stunned on the spot. I ran for it, and I had no money on me, um~~ I was 10 minutes late and got caught red-handed by a school official!

What I can't stand most is those (petty) officials at school, so full of themselves. Sigh~ Today I read an article I'd never known about before, "[Loving Your Country Is Not the Same as Loving the Court](http://www.google.cn/search?q=%E7%88%B1%E5%9B%BD%E5%AE%B6%E4%B8%8D%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%8E%E7%88%B1%E6%9C%9D%E5%BB%B7&hl=zh-CN&source=billboard_xml&cd=12000000&cad=7&ie=utf8)," by Liang Qichao. I still remember Liang Qichao's "On Young China." It has always inspired me!

Choices. The road is mine to choose! I guess I'll have to decide on this soon. Time to think it over quietly.
