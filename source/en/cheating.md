All of a sudden, I felt like I had deceived everyone around me. After that I came to a strange city and hid myself away, then quietly watched everyone. Ever since everyone found out I was going abroad, and I then didn't actually follow through, I haven't wanted to share my whereabouts with anyone anymore. I'm lost, but I don't lack a goal. It's just that this goal wears me out, body and mind. I want to live in a city where nobody knows me, where they don't know my past, and live my own little life.
