> Yesterday I kept a low profile and stayed holed up at home. Today I went out for a stroll~~

This afternoon I went out for a bit. Yesterday I stayed home all day and spent less time online. I think I spent the whole day seriously listening to English, and then in the evening I went online for a little while, which seems to have lasted until 3 this morning, heh<!--more -->

Today is Sunday. We live in a residential area right next to the shopping district, so it's kind of noisy. I hate this kind of environment, but I also seem to depend on it to get by.

I went past my old middle school, which I hadn't been to in a long time. I was just passing by. The school has changed a lot. It's been more than a year since I left middle school, and I miss it but can't go back. Everyone misses the old days and thinks back on the past, but we always have to keep moving forward, look ahead, and stop clinging to the past.

The afternoon sun was lovely, without the glare of summer or the clash of winter and its cold wind. It was so comfortable it made you sleepy. People on the street were out shopping, and I was just passing through, hurrying home from a friend's place.

This afternoon I saw a friend from middle school. He goes to school in another city, so I usually only get to see him during the holidays. This buddy of mine is pretty chubby, haha~
