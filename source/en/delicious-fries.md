Yesterday I went out wandering with a classmate I hadn't seen in months... really just around a few neighborhoods near my house. We watched some people playing basketball, wound through a few alleys, and walked past N little shops, since we had nothing better to do. Benben had gone to Tiandong, which is so far that he didn't come over, so it was just me and my friend wandering around. We bought hot chocolate drinks, and the result was that the hot chocolate upset my stomach... and I threw up as soon as I got home that night...<!-- more -->

Last night I went to the gym because my mom wanted me to check it out... Fine, so I went and had a look around. Not bad at all (the gym membership came free from my dad's work).

Last night I lost my temper with my dad. Never mind, I won't get into it.

Back to today. The title is "Fries Swimming in Ketchup"...
