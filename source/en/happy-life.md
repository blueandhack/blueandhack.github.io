I was going to write about ShowBLOG, but I've pushed it back. Life, Chapter 3: A happy life, a happy me  I'm holed up at home, nothing new going on, just online and more online. Today I checked AdSense and the identity verification I submitted a few days ago went through, so I can finally cash out the 100 bucks in there. Before this I wanted to write a post about what I've learned from building websites…… I can delete and rewrite, rewrite and delete…… it'll need a few more days before it's ready. In a group chat people were discussing what makes the most money. Some said porn sites, some said scraper sites, some said content-stealing proxy sites. Why didn't anyone say, let's make a creative site? A site with real content? An original site? Nope, basically nobody said that. In this age of sharing, everyone has gotten used to getting something for nothing…… scraping, copying, knocking off…… I've gotten a bit sick of this model, and that's exactly why ShowBLOG is coming into being. What the internet brings us is happiness, and so does blogging. My own life flows out into my blog. Since I don't go out much, there's nothing new to write about…… so the blog is updating a lot slower than in the first half of the year…… That's all I've got to say. Happy happy, haha~ I'm pretty cheerful.
