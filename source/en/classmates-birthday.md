Yesterday, well, it counts as yesterday now, was Zheng Yuanjie's birthday, so we had dinner together... Who came~ Zheng Yuanjie, me, Shaofu Ben, JB Pangpang, Gougou, Xizai... that's everyone. It was a lot of fun, and we ate to our hearts' content~<!-- more -->

We ate at a place diagonally across from the Suguo supermarket by the train station~

When we'd nearly finished eating, we started playing rock-paper-scissors. Since some of us aren't good at the game, the penalty was eating onions and drinking beer~~ Haha, I never lost even once. JB Pangpang got stuck eating onions every single time... Gougou got punished too, and had to eat what was left at the bottom of the fried eggplant plate~~~o(∩_∩)o

Before that I went to an internet café, Feiyu. It was pretty good. I barely played any games and spent the whole time working on my blog. I also got kicked out of a C++ QQ group~~ How sad~~ The reason was that I posted a link to my blog. I'll say this: good riddance. It was a chatter group anyway, total garbage.

Today it snowed in Huainan. You could say it's the first time I've seen it: a "peach-blossom snow" in March~ That's really rare. It snowed so hard that in no time the ground was covered in white~

I took these at night behind my house, with a four-story building in front. The snow is almost melted now. Yesterday was well spent. I wonder how the guy taking the TOEFL did. I had class today, so I wasn't online much~~

Happy birthday yesterday, Zheng Yuanjie~~

I think today is Hu Xiao's and Bi Yu's birthday~ and the day after tomorrow is Ding Ke's. How come everyone's birthday is in March~ Happy early birthday to them all~
