The memory of Wenchuan has not yet faded, and now the Yushu earthquake has taken the lives of so many of our compatriots. We can't stop tragedy from coming, but we can use love to build a line of defense for life. The people in the disaster zone are great, and their spirit will live forever. Let's stand together as one and get through this hardship together!

The site has turned black and white to mourn those who died in the Yushu earthquake. The site is in black and white in their memory...
