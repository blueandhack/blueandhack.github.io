It's because I'm busy that I don't feel like I'm getting nothing out of it. I've slowly gotten used to the busy life in the student union, slowly found my place, and then slowly gotten used to that place. We need to know ourselves, and we need to look at everything with patience. The power of waking up: slowly climbing out of the low point, I'm slowly walking my own road. I'm growing up, and I guess you all are too.
