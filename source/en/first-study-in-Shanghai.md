"Corn Mush" Yu Minhong's TOEFL vocabulary book. After an 8-hour train ride, I got lost the moment I walked out of the station and had no idea which bus to take<!-- more -->

A taxi seemed like a bit of a waste, so I set off alone through this city. I got soaked through, with the rain beating down on me. After asking countless passersby and a traffic cop, I really couldn't find my way, so I flagged down two taxis. The first driver didn't know the way, uh~~ I was speechless. Then I flagged down another one, and he more or less knew the way, so I got in, soaking wet, and waited anxiously.

Finally, 20 minutes later, I arrived at Shanghai TV University and went into my room, Room 210.
