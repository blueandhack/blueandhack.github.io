I really like life in Shanghai. Today is my third day here. I've already signed up for a six-month TOEFL prep course, and I met a friend I got to know on the Taisha forum. From now on we're good buddies. The past two days I've been running all over, riding the subway, wandering around. This afternoon we went to People's Square to see a movie, *If You Are the One 2*, and after that we went our separate ways. My train is tomorrow night, a little after 11, so tomorrow night is going to be a long one. It snowed today, which is rare here. When I came to Shanghai around this time last year, it didn't snow. I always feel like Shanghai is warm, and even with the snow today it still feels warm…
