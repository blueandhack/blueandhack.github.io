We never started from the same line, and yet here we are, thrown together. Ten minutes until lights out in the dorm, so I'll keep it short. I just want to stress one thing: not everyone in this world is the same as you. There will always be lowly dogs and premiers. So let others do whatever they want. He can be his dog and I'll be my own person. Everyone has their own ambitions, and forcing people to do what they don't want isn't great. Good night to me.
