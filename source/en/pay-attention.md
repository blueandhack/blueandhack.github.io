I'm about to graduate from high school, but I don't need to stress about the gaokao at all. One small regret in life: I never took the college entrance exam.<!-- more -->

Be devoted in love, if you're not just in it for fun.

Be focused in learning, if you want more than a surface understanding.

PHP, C++, CSS, JavaScript and so on, I want to learn every one of them, and in the end I'm just a newbie who knows a little bit of each.

Focus on learning one thing, and if you're going to do it, be the best at it.

Today at school I knocked every machine on the LAN offline, using a network-cutting tool. Really works. But my own computer froze too. What a fail...
