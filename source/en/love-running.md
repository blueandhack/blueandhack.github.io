After school started I fell in love with running, and now I can't stop. I'm too busy during the day to run, so I do it at 9 p.m., after evening study hall. Every day I run a bit more than the day before, and that's progress. I really like the Endomondo app. Its stats are very detailed, and in this age of big data, information matters a lot. <!-- more -->
