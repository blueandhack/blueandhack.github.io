The Bloggers of the Lei Feng Group

Author
Yujia Lin
Date
February 26, 2011
Reading time
3 min read

Translated from Chinese by Claude. Read the original

Me, I’ve outed my own face before, and now I’ve been outed again…

They used a photo I took around this time last year. I’ve changed quite a bit, so let me post a recent photo of myself

What I want to say is: independent blogs rule…

Lately the blogging scene has been pretty quiet, maybe because of the end of 2010, but the first quarter of 2011 is almost over and the blogging circle is still dead in the water. So it occurred to me that online we may all know each other well, maybe even visit each other’s blogs, leave comments and chat, but if we met in person we might not recognize each other. Even though the Lei Feng crowd (named after Lei Feng, China’s famous model of selfless helpfulness) is spread all over the country, I still hope the bloggers of the Lei Feng group (QQ group: 50698844) can organize more activities and liven up the circle.

Microblogs are all the rage now, and lots of people post what they want to say on microblogs instead, so blogs get updated much less often. So this event is the Lei Feng group’s “truth revealed”: we’re posting good-looking photos of some of the bloggers from the circle here. This is also #Lei Feng group#’s first collective self-outing. Below are the photos of the bloggers in the blogging Lei Feng group (QQ group: 50698844).

Houzi: a blogger who is an IDC host, restaurant owner and foreign trader all in one.

Bi Yang: independent blogger for 5 years, works at follow5.com.

Wan Ge: blogs mostly about wp tips, loves programming, always happy to help.

null

Su Jiong: focused on SEO, formerly ran operations at Zhanzhang Baike, now works at Anqing Net, fifth place in the 4th SEO contest.

Qimi: farming at home, devoted to growing sugarcane, a farmer of the new era dreaming of a yield in the tens of thousands per mu!

null

Yimi: loves every kind of internet technology and every new electronic gadget.

Lin Mumu: passionate about making wordpress themes, and has fun tinkering with wordpress.

Wang Yuming: blogs mainly about following the internet, sharing the internet, and researching promotion and SEO.

null

Chen Xi: UI designer and visual designer with an extreme aesthetic obsession, now works at Tencent.

null

Su Yang: a young writer devoted to the path of Chinese-ism, with delicate posts and a careful personality.

null

Qin Dashao: blogs mostly about sharing and recording life and trying out new digital gadgets, a total gadget geek.

Koukou: independent blog enthusiast, centered on sharing wordpress themes, quality software and classic videos!

null

Laoqi: an immortal, one of the founders of the blogging Lei Feng group, now does R&D at Huawei.

Maoge: calls himself a villager of Yuping Village, one of the clueless masses who has been hanging around the internet for ages.

Zhou Baojun: his blog is mainly about sharing life and work, with the occasional reaction to online events.

Liu Ya: a beautiful blogger focused on researching SEO and online marketing, one of the core members of Mou Changqing’s 28Tui.

blueandhack: a high school student who loves programming, currently absorbed in a blog project and getting ready to study abroad.

Those are the photos of 18 bloggers from the Lei Feng group. This is our first collective self-outing, the list is in no particular order, and please don’t repost this entry without permission. Finally, everyone is welcome to join the #independent blog# Lei Feng group: 50698844, where we hold events from time to time. Lei Feng loves clicking ads, Lei Feng loves commenting, Lei Feng loves answering questions, Lei Feng loves blogging. I hope you’ll become a member of the Lei Feng group too.
